BP Reels — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · A Beyond Pixells product

1. Who we are

BP Reels ("we", "us", "our") is a social media content automation service operated by Beyond Pixells. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, WhatsApp number and email — collected when you message us or sign up.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by our payment provider. We never see or store your card/UPI credentials; we only receive a confirmation that payment succeeded.
  • Social account connections: when you connect Instagram or YouTube, those platforms (Meta / Google) give us a limited access token that permits posting on your behalf. We never see or store your passwords.
  • Service data: your chosen niche, posting schedule, and which reels we posted for you and when.

3. How we use it

  • To post reels to your connected accounts on the schedule you chose.
  • To contact you about your subscription, setup and support.
  • To honour our Consistency Guarantee (tracking posting days per cycle).

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with advertisers. We do not use your data to train AI models.

4. Access tokens

Tokens are stored encrypted, are limited to content publishing only, and can be revoked at any time — by you disconnecting the account, or by you deleting your data (see below). Instagram tokens expire automatically every 60 days and require your re-authorisation.

5. Third parties

We rely on: Meta (Instagram), Google (YouTube), our payment provider, and our website/payment infrastructure providers. Each processes data under its own privacy policy. Links to their policies are available on request.

6. Retention

We keep your data only while your subscription is active, plus 12 months for billing and legal records. After that it is deleted.

7. Your rights

  • Ask what data we hold about you.
  • Ask for corrections.
  • Ask for a copy of your data.
  • Ask for deletion of your data at any time.

To use any of these rights, see our Data Deletion page.

8. Children

Our service is not directed at anyone under 18. Accounts must be owned by adults.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: reach us on WhatsApp, or email support@bpreels.in (placeholder — official contact channel will be confirmed on signup).
