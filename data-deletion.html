Last updated: 7 September 2026 · A Beyond Pixells product

Delete your data — 3 simple steps

You can request deletion of all personal data we hold about you at any time. No forms, no fees, no questions.

1. Email support@bpreels.in with the subject "Delete my data". Include the mobile number or email you signed up with.
2. We confirm your identity (a quick question about your account — no documents needed).
3. Within 7 days we permanently delete your contact details, posting history, and revoke and erase all Instagram/YouTube access tokens. We also cancel any active subscription so you are never charged again.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, mobile number and email address
  • All stored access tokens and platform connections
  • Your niche, schedule and posting records

What we keep (only if legally required)

  • Basic billing records (amount, date) for tax purposes, for the period required by Indian law. These contain no contact details.

Notes

Disconnecting your Instagram/YouTube account in the platform's own settings (Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites, or Google → Security → Third-party access) immediately revokes our access, independent of this process.
